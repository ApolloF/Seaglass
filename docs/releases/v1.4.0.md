WaterLauncher 1.4 brings choosing a game's art from the couch, a controller test, a welcome for new installs and collections, smooth big picture at 4K, and better art and matching for games Steam doesn't sell.

**New**

- **Choose the art**: a game's page in big picture has *Art*: pick its cover, background, banner and logo (L1 / R1 switch between them) from Steam's art and screenshots, the Epic store and PCGamingWiki, and with a SteamGridDB key many more. Your choice stays when details refresh.
- **Test your controller**: *Settings → Test controller* shows every button, stick and trigger live, which buttons have worked, and whether a stick drifts. Hold ✕ to feel each rumble, hold ○ to leave.
- **Collections**: put games in collections of your own ("Playing now", "Co-op", …). They show in desktop mode's sidebar and as Library tabs in big picture (L2 / R2); add games from a game's details or its page in big picture.
- **Welcome**: a new install starts with a short welcome: what was found, where WaterLauncher looks, and how you like to play.

**Smoother**

- Orbit no longer flickers or stutters while you glide through it: at 4K it now draws ten times as many frames and uses half the memory.
- Console and Deck stay smooth with a direction held, also with thousands of games.
- The mouse pointer hides in big picture while you use the controller or keyboard.

**Art and matching**

- Games only Epic sells get their art and details even from a repack or a plain folder (for example Alan Wake 2).
- Controller support comes from PCGamingWiki too, so games that support the DualSense but don't say so on Steam start directly instead of through Steam Input.
- Backgrounds are chosen from several pictures, sharp up to 4K and calm where the text sits. Details and art are fetched once more in the background after updating.
- More folder names are recognised: short names (SkyrimSE, L4D2, AC Valhalla, Mafia 2 DE), names written together, more release groups, and folders named after an expansion. Folders that aren't games (Tools, Redist, launchers) are never matched.

**Fixes**

- A game that crashes says so, with its error, instead of "Welcome back".
- A game that takes a while to load shows *Loading* until its window appears.
- In big picture Search, PageUp / PageDown and Tab work while typing.
- The game page's text is easier to read over bright backgrounds.
