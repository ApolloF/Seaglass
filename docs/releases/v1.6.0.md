**Achievements** for your games, store installs and unofficial copies alike.

**New**

- **Achievements in a game's details**: how many you have, a progress bar and your latest unlocks. *Show all* lists every one, unlocked first, with when you got it and how rare it is.
- **In big picture** too: *Achievements* on a game's page.
- **After you play**, Seaglass says what you unlocked, with the icons.
- **Where they come from**: Steam (from Steam itself; with your Steam Web API key also games Steam hasn't cached), Epic (sign in to Epic to see your progress), GOG (GOG Galaxy's progress; sign in to GOG in *Settings → Accounts* for names and icons) and the files Steam emulators keep for unofficial copies (Goldberg, gbe_fork, EMPRESS, CODEX, RUNE, OnlineFix, CreamAPI, Reloaded, dodi, SKIDROW, SmartSteamEmu, TENOKE, 3DM, ALI213, Razor1911 and Nemirtinga's Epic emulator). How rare an achievement is comes from Steam's global stats, Epic and GOG.
- **Hidden achievements** stay hidden until you unlock them. *Settings → Library → Show hidden achievements* shows them anyway, and the full list can reveal them.

**Notes**

- Achievements are read when you open a game and after you play, never in the background, and nothing is sent anywhere while a game runs.
- Not read yet: EA, Ubisoft, Battle.net and Xbox games, and unofficial copies by VOICES38, CPY, PLAZA, FLT, Steamworks Fix, Hoodlum and DARKSiDERS, whose files aren't publicly described. Sample files are welcome in an issue.
- You can turn achievements off in *Settings → Library*.
