**New**

- **Console games from your emulators.** Seaglass now finds RPCS3 (PS3), Dolphin (GameCube and Wii) and Cemu (Wii U) and the games they can play, and lists them next to your PC games, in desktop mode and every big picture layout. They get their real names, the console as their label, art where SteamGridDB or the game itself has some, and playtime like any other game. Play starts the emulator full screen, and you're back in Seaglass when it closes. The emulator handles your controller itself. Two copies of the same game (two regions) show their serial so you can tell them apart. Turn it off in Settings → Library, or hide the *Consoles* library.
- **Launch options.** In a game's menu, *Launch options…* sets the arguments its program starts with, and optionally the ones that make it close the game again. With *Choose program…* you can start a game through a script or another launcher.
