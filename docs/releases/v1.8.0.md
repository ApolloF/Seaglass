**Syncer accounts**: each person on a PC gets their own saves, playtime, achievements and settings, on every PC they play on.

**New**

- **Who's playing.** With *Accounts* on in Syncer, games play as the person Syncer has on this PC, without asking. To switch, use the sidebar (desktop), *Who's playing* in quick access (big picture) or Settings → Saves. Syncer puts that person's saves in place.
- **Playtime, last played and achievements go with the person.** They're kept per person and added up over all their PCs. Achievements unlocked on another PC show up here too.
- **Settings go with the person** (*Same settings on every PC*): appearance, controller, while playing, saves, achievements, updates and which libraries show. Game folders, store sign-ins and *start in big picture* stay per PC.
- **Syncer syncs and backs them up** (*Sync them with Syncer*, on). This needs **Syncer 0.14** or newer. An older Syncer still switches accounts, and Seaglass tells you to update it.
- **Ask who's playing** (off, per PC): for a PC people take turns on, a game asks whose saves to use before it starts.

**Changes**

- The library's playtime and last played now come from your profile. What this PC had before is carried over once, the first time you start this version.
