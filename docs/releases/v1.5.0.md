WaterLauncher is now **Seaglass**. This update installs Seaglass in place of WaterLauncher and brings your library, playtime, settings, art and "start with Windows" along. Seaglass is now licensed under the AGPL-3.0.

**New**

- **Install Syncer with one click**: *Settings → Saves* (and a game's saves card) installs Syncer, or updates it, straight from its GitHub releases. The download is checked against the checksum GitHub published for it. *About Syncer* opens its project page.
- **Hide libraries**: turn off whole libraries you don't want to see, such as Xbox, unofficial copies or plain folders, in *Settings → Library* or big picture's Settings. Their games still count playtime.
- **Orbit** puts the games you've played lately and your favorites in and around the middle.

**Fixes**

- The PS button in big picture no longer takes the window out of full screen.

**Changed**

- Add-ons (DLSS Updater) are no longer part of Seaglass for now. DLSS Updater keeps working on its own.
- Seaglass installs to `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\Seaglass` and keeps its data in `%APPDATA%\Seaglass` and `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Seaglass`. Shortcuts you made yourself that start `WaterLauncher.exe` (for example with `--play`) need to point at `Seaglass.exe`.
