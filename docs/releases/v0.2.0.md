Real art and details for your games.

**Metadata and art**

- Covers, hero banners, logos and icons, plus description, developer, release year and genres, fetched in the background as soon as games are found (recently played first).
- Steam provides art and details for every game with a Steam AppID, which includes external copies whose emulator carries the AppID. No account or key needed.
- GOG games get GOG's banner, icon and description.
- Games the game database doesn't know are looked up on the Steam store by their exact title.
- Optional: add your own free SteamGridDB API key in Settings, Library to fill in art for games Steam and GOG don't have. The key is stored encrypted for your Windows account.
- Every image is downloaded only from Steam, GOG or SteamGridDB, then checked, resized and re-encoded before it's stored; the interface only ever loads images from WaterLauncher itself.
- Each game gets an accent colour taken from its art.

**Getting the right game**

- **Change game…** (in the ⋯ menu, and on games that need a check): search the Steam store and pick the right game. Your choice is kept across rescans.
- **Refresh details and art** fetches everything again.

**Controller support detection**

- WaterLauncher now knows whether a game supports DualSense natively, from Steam's store data and from the game's own files (Sony's DualSense library or SDL). The details panel shows what Auto will do; it takes effect with launching in v0.4.

Art is cached in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\WaterLauncher\art`.
