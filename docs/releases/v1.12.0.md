**New**

- **Big picture for game streaming.** Start Seaglass with `--bigpicture` to open big picture just this once, for example as the app a Sunshine or Moonlight stream starts. Your settings don't change. Give the stream `--leave-bigpicture` as its undo command, and Seaglass goes back to how it was when the stream ends: back in the tray, or back on the desktop.
